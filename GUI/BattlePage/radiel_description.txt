High Arbiter of the Stranger’s Realm.
Within this desolate dominion, no trespasser escapes the grasp of the ever-watchful Clowns. Any who dare to cross the borders are dragged before a grotesque tribunal, a court forged of circus tents where fairness is but an illusion.
The young Arbiter bears no disguise for her malice—her scorn toward outsiders is unrelenting. Few survive her judgment to be welcomed among the ranks of the Clowns.
The condemned, denied passage, meet two fates: stripped of all they own and cast back beyond the borders… or shackled as slaves, condemned to the lowest of labors.
Yet, in fleeting moments of discourse, one might glimpse in her eyes a fragile spark—an innocence and kindness long buried, yet befitting the age she truly bears.
